Gunter Ecom

Project Overview

Gunter Ecom is an online shop for Gunter's handmade medieval armor and weapons, also with a private to-do list where Gunter tracks the orders he has to forge. Customers browse three product lines, Nuremberg, Greenwich and Milan, where they can also filter and sort through them. Gunter can log in and check all of the orders he has to do. Customers can also check on their live order status after they have placed an order

Usage Guide

Customers

1. Start on the home page, select one of the three lines to start browsing
2. Filter and sort through the products to find one you like
3. If you find one you like, click on it and press add to cart
4. Navigate to the cart screen (either through the task bar or the cart button in the top right)\
5. Add more of the same item if you want
6. Fill out your name and any notes about the item. Press place order
7. Once you press place order, Gunter will start working and you can track the status of your order by clicking My Orders

Gunter

1. Go the login page (either through the task bar or the user icon in the top right)
2. Fill out correct email and password and press login
3. Once you are in the Gunter page, you can see a list of all of the orders
4. Once you start forging, change the status to forging, and once the product is finshed, change the status to ready
5. Once the status is ready that order will disappear
6. After you are done completing orders, you can log out

Feature List

1. Navbar to access the rest of the website
2. Accessible buttons in the top right for the Home page, the Cart Page, and the Login Page
3. Shop the lines button that brings you to where to the lines
4. 3 different lines each with 15 products
5. A sort and filter button
6. Add to cart button
7. Option for placing notes for Gunter
8. Removing and increasing the quantity in the cart
9. Placing an order
10. My Orders page that shows the status of all your orders
11. Login page for Gunter
12. Gunter can change status of any order
